Attention — shadow, NOT a buy signal

  

    S&P names whose English Wikipedia page gained the most readers: the median, over the last
    12 complete months, of each month's views over the same month a year before (VIEWS YoY). The
    pool is the 182 names the probe mapped to an article. Graded on history, this top third beat the
    pool by +14.7pp over 3 years and LOST −10.8pp over 5, so it reverses: it never feeds BUY% or the
    rankings above, and an uploaded settings.yaml does not change it. The 5-year read rests on less
    than one independent window (pageviews start in 2015), so neither the reversal nor any fix for it
    is settled yet.
  

  

  
  
Berkshire holdings — shadow, NOT a buy signal

  

    Every stock in Berkshire Hathaway's newest 13F. A 13F lists US-listed holdings only, so its
    Japanese trading houses and its wholly owned companies are not here. WEIGHT is the holding's
    share of the 13F's reported value. STATUS compares it with the quarter before: new, added (more
    than 5% more shares), trimmed (below 95% of the shares) or held (a forward split counts as held),
    and SHARES Δ is that change. BOUGHT is the newest quarter it was bought in over the last 4, or —.
    A 13F is filed up to 45 days after its quarter ends, so the table is already weeks old. ETFs are
    left out. A name the screen does not price shows OpenFIGI's upper-case issuer name. Every holding
    is listed whatever the row count above. It never feeds BUY% or the rankings above, and an
    uploaded settings.yaml does not change it.
  

  

  
  
Camillo videos — shadow, NOT a buy signal

  

    Stocks named in the titles and descriptions of the newest uploads (about 15, roughly one week) on
    Dumb Money Live, Chris Camillo's YouTube channel. A stock matches when the first word of its name
    appears as a whole word in the same capitalisation, so "Amazon" counts but "amazon" in sponsor
    boilerplate does not. A mention is not a buy: he discusses stocks he shorts or avoids too, and a
    company that shares its first word with another can be named by mistake. VIDEOS counts the
    uploads that name it; NEWEST and TITLE are the latest of them. It never feeds BUY% or the rankings
    above, and an uploaded settings.yaml does not change it.
